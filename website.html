Cancellation Policy

When Can You Cancel an Order?

We protect both our customers and riders with fair, common-sense cancellation rules.

FREE CANCELLATION ALLOWED

While status is ORDER_PLACED or RIDER_ASSIGNED. You can freely cancel if plans change.

CANCELLATION LOCKED

Once status advances to ITEM_PURCHASED or ON_THE_WAY. This prevents riders from losing money spent at local stores. Please call the rider directly for urgent changes.

Customer Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about using ZIPPO in Melapalayam.

Do I need to create an account or password to place an order?

No! ZIPPO operates in guest customer mode. All you need is your valid 10-digit mobile number so the assigned rider can call you to confirm your location.

How much does delivery cost? Are there hidden convenience fees?

There are zero convenience fees and zero commission markups. Base fare is flat ₹30 covering the first 2 km inside Melapalayam, plus ₹10/km for extra distance. If you toggle "Urgent Priority", ₹15 is added for instant, dedicated non-batched delivery.

What if the item in Buy For Me is out of stock at the shop?

The assigned rider will call your phone directly from the shop to ask for a substitute, or close the order with a clear note. If closed, you receive an immediate alert and pay nothing.

How do I contact customer care or supervisor support?

You can call our Melapalayam supervisor directly at 7639483476 or 6379329930, or tap the WhatsApp button inside your order tracking sheet.
