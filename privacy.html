Privacy Policy for ZIPPO Express

1 Introduction & Overview

At ZIPPO ("we", "our", or "us"), we are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy discloses our privacy practices and explains how we collect, store, process, and protect your information when you use the ZIPPO mobile application and associated web interfaces.

ZIPPO operates a hyper-local errand and delivery platform serving Melapalayam, Tirunelveli, and surrounding neighborhoods. By downloading, accessing, or using the application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2 Information We Collect

To provide reliable on-demand delivery and errand services, we collect only necessary information:

A. Personal & Contact Information

When registering or placing an order, we collect your Full Name and Mobile Phone Number. This information is required for account authentication, dispatch communications, and order status updates.

B. Precise & Approximate Location Data

With your permission, we collect precise and approximate location coordinates from your device when you set pickup/drop-off points, calculate delivery distance, and track live order progress. Location data is used strictly for delivery fulfillment and is never sold or used for ad targeting.

C. Errand & Order Details

Details of items you request (e.g. food items, medicines, documents, package specifications) and recipient contact details provided during checkout to facilitate accurate errand execution.

D. Device Information & Push Tokens

We collect device model, operating system version, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push notification tokens to ensure real-time delivery milestone alerts (such as "Rider Assigned" and "Out for Delivery").

3 How We Use Your Information

  • Order Dispatch & Delivery: Connecting you with authorized delivery riders to pick up and deliver your packages.
  • Fare Calculation: Accurately calculating distance-based delivery charges and transparent rates.
  • Real-Time Tracking: Displaying order progress from initial booking to handover.
  • Handover Verification: Generating secure 4-digit verification PINs to guarantee packages are handed only to authorized recipients.
  • Customer Support: Resolving queries, tracking feedback, and facilitating phone coordination between rider and customer.

4 Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers. We only share information with:

  • Assigned Delivery Riders: Riders receive only the customer name, delivery address, items to be procured/delivered, and contact number necessary to complete the delivery.
  • Infrastructure Service Providers: Encrypted hosting and database providers (such as Render and MongoDB Atlas) operating under strict security agreements.
  • Legal Authorities: If required by Indian law, legal subpoena, or court order to protect against fraudulent activities or threats to public safety.

5 Data Security & Encryption

We employ industry-standard security measures including Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption for all communication between the mobile application and backend servers. Database records are secured with strict access controls and role-based permissions.

6 Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active or as required to fulfill service requests. You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion:

You can request complete deletion of your account, phone number, saved addresses, and active order records by visiting our online portal or emailing our support team.

ZIPPO Express is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately to have it purged.
