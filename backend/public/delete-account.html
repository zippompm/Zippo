2 Data Retention & Deletion Scope

× What is Permanently Deleted:

  • User profile details (Name, Registered Mobile Number).
  • Saved delivery addresses and GPS coordinate tags.
  • Active push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Customer communication preferences.

⚙ Data Retained for Legal Compliance:

In accordance with Indian commercial, taxation (GST), and accounting compliance laws, completed transactional order receipts and payment records are maintained for statutory audit periods. These historical audit logs are anonymized and cannot be used for any marketing or contact purposes.

Deletion Timeline: Upon receiving your request, your profile is immediately deactivated. All personal identifiers are permanently scrubbed from active operational databases within 7 business days.
